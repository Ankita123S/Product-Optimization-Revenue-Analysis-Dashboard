# Product-Optimization-Revenue-Analysis-Dashboard
Power BI dashboard for analyzing product performance and revenue contribution in a coffee retail business.
☕ Afficionado Coffee Roasters – Product Optimization & Revenue Analysis Dashboard

📌 Project Overview

Developed an interactive Power BI dashboard to analyze product performance, revenue distribution, customer purchasing patterns, and menu optimization opportunities for Afficionado Coffee Roasters. The dashboard provides actionable insights to support strategic business decisions and improve overall profitability.

🎯 Business Objectives

- Identify top revenue-generating products and categories.
- Analyze product popularity and sales performance.
- Evaluate revenue concentration across different product segments.
- Recommend menu optimization strategies to improve operational efficiency and profitability.

📊 Key Features

- Designed KPI cards to monitor Total Quantity Sold (214.47K), Total Revenue (₹698.81K), Average Revenue (₹8.74K), Product Efficiency (₹8.98K), and Revenue Concentration (1.03).
- Developed interactive slicers for Product Type, Product Category, and Store Location to enable dynamic analysis.
- Identified Hero Products based on revenue contribution, transaction frequency, and product ranking.
- Performed Top Revenue Generating Product Analysis to highlight best-performing menu items.
- Created Revenue Contribution Analysis by Category to understand product category performance.
- Conducted Product Popularity vs Revenue Analysis to identify high-demand and high-value products.
- Built Top 10 Cumulative Revenue Product Analysis to evaluate product concentration and business dependency.

💡 Key Insights

✅ Coffee category contributed the highest share of total revenue.

✅ A small number of products generated the majority of revenue, indicating a Pareto (80/20) distribution.

✅ Several products exhibited low sales performance, presenting opportunities for menu optimization.

✅ High dependency on a limited set of products highlighted the importance of portfolio diversification.

🚀 Business Recommendations

Menu Optimization Strategy

- Remove or redesign underperforming products.
- Focus marketing efforts on high-revenue items.
- Reduce menu complexity to improve operational efficiency.

Merchandising Strategy

- Promote newly launched products through targeted campaigns.
- Bundle low-selling items with best-selling products.
- Optimize pricing strategies for high-volume products.
